Lesson flow:
This lesson is designed to have the teachers skip count by the denomination of a coin with the intent of re-enforcing the values attributed to each coin denomination as well as introducing the strategy to count by denominations before summing up the totals to arrive at a final answer.

The Instruction mode takes the user step by step:
1) Introduce the task
2) Focus the student on the worth of the coin
3) Then focus the student on the number of coins
4) Apply these two concepts to the skip count grid to arrive at a worth for the set of like-denomination coins.

Additionally the lesson sequence is setup to bridge the skip counting for quarters by preceding it with a count from the composite set of other coins (e.g. 5 nickels are 25 cents, 1 quarter is 25 cents. 5 dimes are 50 cents, 2 quarters are 50 cents)


Remediation:
1) The student may simply count the number of coins rather than summing the worth of the coins. Four nickels is counted as 4 cents
2) The student may misattribute the worth of a coin: Four nickels are counted as 40 cents (misattributed a dime's worth to a nickel)
3) The student may have miscounted the number of coins: Four nickels are counted 3 (or 5 times) 
4) The student may have failed to transfer the value from the skip count grid to the text input (i.e they mistyped or got distracted between steps).
