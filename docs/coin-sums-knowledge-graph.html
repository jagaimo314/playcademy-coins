Coin Sums Knowledge Graph

I’ve opted to represent the Knowledge Graph as a chart with a forked focus on two concepts that come together to achieve the goal. The Knowledge Graph begins with concrete steps related to building out a child’s numeral and counting knowledge. On one side of the fork the child then builds out arithmetic skill that leads to counting 4 two digit numbers (a CC standard for second graders). On the other side of the fork is building out new vocabulary, mathematical skills, and applying counting strategies to currency, ultimately ending with the ability to sum the value of four different denominations. This skill combined with the skill to add 4 two digit numbers achieves the final goal of summing a set of coins.

Note: Another path of this graph exists by removing the addition path, and building the ability to skip count by unlike denominations (i.e. skip count by quarters, then by dimes, then by nickels, and finally by pennies to arrive at the final answer). This strategy is valid but may be limited in the long term, so I elected to highlight the path that uses a deeper math foundation to arrive at the final result

Sources

Where the references come from

Codes in the tables above resolve here. LT lines are levels from the Clements & Sarama learning-trajectories work — developmental levels observed in children, each mapped by their team to the CCSS codes beside them.

  • CCSS — Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, grades K–2: K.CC, K.OA, 1.OA, 1.NBT, 2.OA, 2.NBT, 2.MD. Money appears exactly once in K–2, at 2.MD.C.8.
  • TEKS — Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills, mathematics. §111.2 Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2. Cited because TEKS splits coin work into steps CCSS leaves implicit.
  • VA SOL — Virginia 2023 Mathematics Standards of Learning; 1.NS.1(f) names coin identification “by their attributes.”
  • LT — Clements & Sarama, Learning and Teaching with Learning Trajectories (Marsico Institute, University of Denver), with their alignment of every level to CCSS and the subitizing trajectory.
